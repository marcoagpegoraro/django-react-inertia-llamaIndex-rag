Pulseboard Company Policy

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Core collaboration hours run from 10:00 to 16:00 UTC on workdays. Longer-term remote arrangements should be reviewed with the team lead once per quarter.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO requests should be submitted at least 10 calendar days in advance. Requests for more than 10 consecutive business days need both manager approval and People Operations approval.

## Travel and expenses
Flights and hotels should be booked through the approved travel portal whenever possible. Travel reimbursement above $500 requires manager pre-approval before the expense is incurred. Expense reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days after returning from travel. Meals are reimbursed up to $65 per day and alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Security and data handling
Customer data may only be stored in company-approved systems. Files that contain customer data must never be sent through personal email or consumer file-sharing tools. Security incidents and suspected phishing attempts must be reported to the security team within one hour.

## Equipment and home office
New hires may request up to $750 for home office equipment during their first 90 days. Standard laptop replacements are available every three years unless a manager approves an earlier replacement for support or performance reasons. Equipment purchases above the stipend limit need department approval.

## Learning and development
Each employee receives a $1,200 annual learning stipend for courses, books, and conferences related to their role. Conference travel using the stipend still follows the travel approval rules. Reimbursement requests for learning expenses should be submitted within 30 days.
